06 / 06 Fragrance pop-up for Gucci

Sensorial Dive

“How deep are you willing to go?”

ProjectFragrance pop-up for Gucci
LocationImmersive pop-up
ScopeConcept, spatial design, experience
Concept wordswater flow, reflection, botanical, structural
01 The brief

Sensorial Dive was born from a single question: what if a fragrance were not something you wore, but somewhere you went?

02 The idea

A corridor that pulls you forward, a light that beckons from the end, a room that blooms open like a secret. An immersive world built around the most intimate thing Gucci makes, and you are the one diving in.

water flow
reflection
botanical
structural
1 / 4 03 Concept words
04 Moodboard

The references move between the botanical and the surreal: water flow and structural glass, reflection and dissolution, the precision of a bottle and the abandon of a garden in full bloom.

water flow reflection botanical structural
05 Visualisations

From concept to space

A digital screen of nature, water and Gucci aesthetic. A botanical plinth for each fragrance, with a bespoke floral arrangement that mirrors its notes. And at the corridor's end, a beckoning purple light that invites guests to submerge themselves in the brand's story.

SENSORIAL DIVE
06 The result
Nature here is not decoration, it is architecture. Purple bleeds into pink bleeds into the memory of a scent you cannot name but have always known.
