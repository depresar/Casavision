About the atelier

Every project begins with a conversation and one strong idea. That idea runs through everything: the space, the brand, the event.

CASAVISION is a creative atelier based in Dubai, working across the UAE and internationally. We bring an Italian design sensibility: the kind that respects the past, understands the present and builds for what comes next.

Each project starts with research into the audience, the client, the brand and the market. The design follows those findings rather than trends or ready-made templates.

How we work

From a conversation to the first opening

You should see how we think, not only the pictures. Every case on this site follows the same path.

  1. 01

    Conversation

    We start by listening: who you are, what you are launching and who it is for.

  2. 02

    Research

    We study the audience, the client, the brand and the market. The design follows these findings.

  3. 03

    Concept

    One strong idea and a story that belongs to this project only.

  4. 04

    Moodboard

    Images, materials and the key words of the concept, like stillness, desert, oasis.

  5. 05

    Design

    Space, identity and visualisations developed around that one idea.

  6. 06

    Realisation

    We stay with the project through to the opening, the event or the first point of sale.

What we believe in

Design is not what you see. It is what you feel inside it.

Human First

Every brief begins not with dimensions, but with understanding who will live inside the work. People are not the audience. They are the origin.

Collective Intelligence

The atelier is a meeting point. Designers, clients, artisans and collaborators think together, because the best ideas are never the product of a single mind.

Cultural Depth

Rooted in Italian design culture, we draw from history and craft, not from trends. Our work is grounded, but never static.

Conviction in Detail

Nothing in a CASAVISION project is accidental. Every material, every object, every line is a position taken.

Vision for Longevity

We design for the people who will inhabit a space in twenty years, not just the ones who commission it today.

Warmth & Rigour

The care of a home and the precision of an atelier, always together. Emotion and craft are the same thing, seen differently.

Design by people. For people. Built to last.
