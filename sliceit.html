SLICEit

“Just the good part.”

ProjectPizza pop-up
LocationDubai
ScopeConcept, naming, identity, pop-up design
Concept wordsyoung, bold, diverse, contemporary
01 The brief

SLICEit was born from the most honest craving in the world: a single, perfect slice, hot from a wood-fired oven, eaten standing up, no occasion required.

02 The idea

In a city that does everything at scale, we asked a different question: what if the best pizza in Dubai came in one piece, from a window, in under a minute?

04 Moodboard

Fire, steel, and a slice that speaks for itself. Every detail is turned up: the red is redder, the crust is louder.

young bold diverse contemporary
05 Visualisations

From concept to space

Bold red on white, a logotype with personality to spare, and product shots so unapologetic they make you hungry before you finish looking. The identity was built to stop people mid-scroll and mid-street.

SLICEIT
06 The result
Designed to be remembered after a single visit. This is not fast food. This is fast confidence.
